How a connector finds its home

The rule from the connectors call of 4 November: a connector only one provider has goes to that provider. A connector both have is tested on real accounts and picked against what the families asked for. A connector neither has needs Swayu's own adapter or a paid partner. Every family then reaches it through the same gateway, with its own permissions.

Sources: the Swayu connectors survey, answers of 2 November 2026 from Dhruv, HRB, Maanas and Sujit; Composio's live tool search and Zernio's published platform list, both read on 4 November 2026; Lighthouse measurements on 4 November 2026.
