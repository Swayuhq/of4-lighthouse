One line for every customer message

Where the customer message line stands in the Lighthouse prototype, what is proven, and where the OF3 sources (website contact forms and Google profile questions) join it. Written so OF3 and OF4 can build on one line, not two.

1 · Two lines, not one

The incoming line and the outgoing line

Incoming line mostly built

Every customer message is saved first, in one place, before anything else happens. Nothing is lost if Swayu is slow or a platform is down. This is the queue discussed on 22 October, and the one OF3 sources join.

Outgoing line not built

Replies leaving Swayu wait their turn under the provider's limit instead of being refused. It belongs to the connector layer. OF3 needs nothing from it beyond knowing it exists.

5 · Building it together

Where OF3 plugs in

What a source needs to provide

Every message in the line has the same few fields (InboxMessage in src/parse.ts). A new source only has to fill them; the line adds the business when it saves.

FieldMeaning
idThe source's own id, so a resend is saved once
platformWhere it came from: website form, Google question
authorThe customer's name as given
receivedAtWhen they wrote it
textWhat they wrote
replyRefHow to answer: an email, a question id, or none
linkThe message's own page, when there is one

Questions for OF3

  1. How does an O68 contact form message arrive: a post to a Swayu address, an email, or something else?
  2. Can the owner reply to a form message from Swayu, or is it read only?
  3. Google profile questions: will they come through Zernio's Google connection, the one that already carries reviews?
  4. Is 9 November still the date for the Google Business Profile connection?
  5. Any other incoming customer message in the OF3 plan?

What OF4 brings

  • The line, the intake checks and the 30 day rule, ready to receive a new channel
  • The inbox the owner already uses
  • One Google connection for reviews and questions, not two
