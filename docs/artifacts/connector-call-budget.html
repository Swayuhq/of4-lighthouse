2 · The path of every request

One front desk between every family and every outside account

No family calls Instagram or Google directly. Every request goes through one front desk that does four checks in order. Think of a bank with one counter per outside account: the desk joins identical requests, lets urgent customers go first, makes sure no single business takes every slot, and never hands out more slots than the bank allows.

What already holdsSteps 1, 2 for a single queue, and 4 run today inside one Lighthouse app: one budget per provider, an atomic take, a 429 pauses every caller, ten identical reads cost one. Verified 5 November, 208 tests passing.
The gapEach app keeps its own count, but Zernio counts the whole team. swayu-app, Lighthouse and the ads tools each believe they have 600 a minute. The budget must move to one shared table, and steps 2 and 3 need the three lanes and the fair share.

7 · To confirm with Dhruv

Six decisions that close the concurrency question

  1. One Zernio team and one Composio organisation for every family, so one shared budget table can count them.
  2. The front desk as code every app imports, with the budget in one Postgres table, or one separate gateway service every family calls.
  3. Three lanes in this order: Send, owner looking, background. A background job never delays a reply.
  4. Inbox moves to push before beta, with the 10 minute check as the safety net.
  5. Who pays the per message and per location items: Swayu, or passed to the owner. And which keyword service, since OF1, OF3 and OF5 all need one.
  6. Who owns the daily usage count by family, provider and minute, read from the ledger row every gateway call already writes. It replaces the survey estimates on this page with real numbers.
